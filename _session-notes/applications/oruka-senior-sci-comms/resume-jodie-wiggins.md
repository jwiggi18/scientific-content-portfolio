## Profile

PhD biologist with ten-plus years across scientific writing, content design, and editorial leadership in the life sciences. I translate genetics, genomics, physiology, and evolution for the people who actually use it — clinicians, learners, scientific reviewers, K–12 educators — without flattening the uncertainty that's really there. I'm strongest where complex evidence has to land cleanly with audiences who'll use it to make decisions, and where the system to deliver it has to be built before it can be used.
